What We Do

Four trades, one contractor

Most of our clients need more than one of these at the same site — a borehole with a solar pump, or a new build wired and fitted with cameras before handover. We plan them together so nothing gets rebuilt twice.

Borehole Drilling

We survey the site, drill and case the borehole, and test the yield before we hand it over — so you know what you're getting before you rely on it.

  • Depths up to 200 metres
  • 152mm–200mm steel casing
  • Hydrogeological survey included
  • Test pumping & water quality check
  • NEMA / EIA permit assistance

Solar Power Systems

Submersible solar pumps for sites with no grid power, plus panel and inverter installations sized to what you're actually running — a pump, a home, or a small business.

  • Submersible solar borehole pumps
  • Panel & inverter sizing
  • Battery backup options
  • Borehole-to-tank solar pumping
  • System maintenance plans

CCTV & Security

Camera systems for gates, compounds, warehouses, and offices — set up with remote viewing on your phone before we leave the site.

  • HD & night-vision cameras
  • Remote viewing on your phone
  • Gate & perimeter coverage
  • DVR / NVR storage
  • Ongoing support

Electrical, Plumbing & Construction

General electrical wiring, plumbing, and construction works — usually bundled into a borehole or solar project, but available on their own too.

  • Wiring & fault-finding
  • Water reticulation & plumbing
  • Tank stands & foundations
  • Site electrical for pump systems
