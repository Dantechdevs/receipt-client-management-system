About Us

Built on Fieldwork, Not Just Promises

We're a Kenyan contracting team working across borehole drilling, solar power, and CCTV security — led on-site, not just quoted from an office.

What We Do

One contractor for water, power, and security

Jamarian Power Solution is led on-site by James Mutie, covering borehole drilling, solar-powered pumping, CCTV installation, electrical work, plumbing, and construction. Most of our jobs combine more than one of these — a new borehole paired with a solar pump, or a home wired and fitted with cameras before the family moves in.

  • Borehole drilling and casing up to 200 metres
  • Solar-powered submersible pumping systems
  • CCTV and security camera installation
  • Electrical wiring and fault-finding
  • Plumbing and water reticulation
  • General construction and tank stands
Why Clients Choose Us

A single point of contact

Drilling a borehole, then finding a separate solar contractor, then a separate security company, means three different quotes, three different timelines, and nobody responsible for how the pieces fit together. We handle the whole site.

  • One team for drilling, solar, and security
  • Water tested on-site before we leave
  • NEMA / EIA permit paperwork handled for you
  • Every job documented with a signed quotation, invoice, and receipt
Our Approach

How a project actually runs

1. Site visit & survey

We visit the site, run a hydrogeological survey where drilling is involved, and confirm exactly what's needed before quoting.

2. Quotation & agreement

You get a written quotation with pricing, timeline, and payment terms — and a signed agreement before mobilization.

3. Drilling & installation

Drilling, casing, solar equipment, or CCTV installation is carried out on-site by our team, not subcontracted out.

4. Testing & handover

Test pumping, water quality checks, and system walkthroughs happen before we hand over — with an invoice and receipt for your records.
